How It Began

From a family kitchen to Pune's favourite table

The Urban Plate began as a Sunday ritual — friends and family gathered around a long table, plates passed hand to hand, stories shared over food cooked with love.

In 2015, we turned that ritual into a restaurant. We wanted a place that felt premium but never stuffy; where a family of six, a couple on their first date and a team celebrating a launch could all feel equally at home. Today, our kitchen brings together the comforting flavours of India with the best of Continental, Italian and Asian cooking — all under one roof.

Food Philosophy

Cook Honestly. Serve Generously.

We believe food tastes best when it is made from scratch, seasoned with patience and shared with people you love. That's the only rule in our kitchen.

Our Mission

To serve consistently delicious, thoughtfully prepared food in a warm and welcoming space — making every visit, big or small, feel like an occasion worth remembering.

Our Vision

To be Pune's most loved premium casual dining destination — known for quality without pretension, and hospitality that feels genuinely personal.
